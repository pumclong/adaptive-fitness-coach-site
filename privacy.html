Policy record / Release candidate

Privacy, in plain language.

Adaptive Fitness Coach is designed to keep fitness information on the user’s device. It does not require an account and does not send workout data to a developer-operated service.

01 / Scope

What this policy covers

This policy describes the Adaptive Fitness Coach iPhone app and this public support website. “The developer,” “we,” and “us” refer to the app’s publisher. The app is currently in release preparation; this policy is provided for transparency and App Store review readiness.

The app is built for offline, local use. Core planning, workout guidance, history, and deterministic progression functions do not require an account, a developer server, or cloud AI.

02 / Stored on device

Information the app may store locally

The exact record depends on what the user chooses to enter and complete:

Category Examples Purpose
Profile and plan Fitness goals, schedule, duration, experience, preferences Create and edit a relevant training plan
Gym setup Available equipment, machine identity, units, confirmed increments Choose suitable exercises and make comparable records
Workout record Exercises, sets, repetitions, load, duration, substitutions, completion Guide sessions, show history, and prepare reviewable suggestions
Optional check-in Body weight, waist measurement, external cardio minutes, notes Show user-requested context without making it required

This information is held in app storage on the device. It is not automatically transmitted to the developer.

03 / Not collected

No account, ads, analytics, or sensor access

The release candidate does not include or use:

  • An account system or developer-operated cloud synchronization
  • Advertising networks, cross-app tracking, or analytics SDKs
  • Cloud AI for workout operation, coaching, or progression decisions
  • Apple Health or HealthKit integration
  • Location services
  • Camera or microphone access

Because the app does not operate a data-collection service, the developer does not sell app fitness data or use it for targeted advertising.

04 / Backup and export

Manual, user-directed backup

The app’s AsyncStorage data is configured to be excluded from automatic iOS device backup. The app does not automatically store or synchronize fitness records in iCloud.

Users can create an export and choose its destination through the iOS Files interface. That destination may be local storage, an external provider, or a cloud folder the user selects. Once exported, the file is governed by the user’s choices and the selected provider’s privacy and security terms—not by this app.

Private fitness data: an export can contain profile, plan, workout-history, machine, and optional measurement information. Store it securely and share it only when intended.

A temporary recovery copy may be placed in system cache. iOS can purge cached files, so a cache copy is not a durable backup. Use a deliberate export for longer-term preservation.

05 / External services

Exercise links leave the app’s local boundary

Some exercise guidance may link to professionally reviewed demonstrations hosted by another website. Opening an external link can disclose ordinary connection information—such as IP address, browser details, and request time—to that destination. Its own privacy policy and terms apply.

The workout remains usable offline with included text and available local media fallbacks. External demonstrations are optional and are not required while performing an active set.

This static support website does not contain analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, external fonts, or executable scripts. GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, may process ordinary server logs under GitHub’s privacy statement.

06 / User control

The record belongs to the user

  • Entering optional body measurements, external cardio, and notes is voluntary.
  • The user chooses when and where to export a backup.
  • Deleting the app removes its on-device app data according to iOS behavior.
  • An exported backup remains at its chosen destination until the user removes it there.

Before reinstalling the app or changing devices, create a manual export if the history should be retained.

07 / Fitness and safety

General adult fitness—not medical advice

Adaptive Fitness Coach is intended for adults using it for general fitness. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose a condition, and does not provide medical treatment or emergency services. It is not a replacement for a qualified clinician or exercise professional.

Exercise instruction, safety wording, and progression rules require qualified professional review before public release. The app does not infer medical status or use the camera to score form.

08 / Changes and contact

Questions or privacy concerns

This policy may be updated when the app’s features, distribution, or legal requirements change. A material update will be reflected by a revised effective date on this page.

To ask a privacy question or report a concern, open a ticket through Adaptive Fitness Coach Support on GitHub Issues. Do not include workout history, body measurements, medical information, exported backups, or other sensitive personal data in a public issue.
