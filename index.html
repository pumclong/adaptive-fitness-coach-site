FIELD NOTE / 01

The intended experience

The plan stays flexible. The workout stays focused.

The app separates planning from doing. A user can revise future sessions when needed, while the active workout remains a stable record of what was planned and completed.

01 / PREPARE

Build around the person

Goals, available time, experience, preferences, and confirmed gym equipment shape the plan.

02 / TRAIN

Guide the next action

Warm-up, resistance work, aerobic work, and a short summary are presented as one complete session.

03 / ADAPT

Suggest, never silently apply

Progression proposals are reviewable. The user can apply, keep, or edit each proposed change.

Privacy by structure

Your training record stays with you.

The release candidate is local-first and works without an account. Workout operation and plan history do not depend on a developer cloud or cloud AI.

See the complete privacy policy

  • ✓No account, advertising, or analytics SDK
  • ✓No HealthKit, location, or camera access
  • ✓No automatic cloud sync or cloud AI
  • ✓Manual export goes only to a destination the user chooses
FIELD NOTE / 02

Responsible release

General fitness guidance—not medical care.

Adaptive Fitness Coach is intended for adult general-fitness use. It does not diagnose conditions, provide medical treatment, or replace advice from a qualified health or exercise professional.

General guidance: exercise instruction, safety language, and progression rules are general fitness information. They are not a prescription or medical advice.
